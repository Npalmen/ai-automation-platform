# Human review rubric — digital coworker reply quality

Version: `coworker_human_review_v1`

## Dimensions (1–5)

1. Sounds like a competent coworker
2. Progresses the case
3. Asks useful questions
4. Specific without overpromising
5. Clear and natural

## Gate R2 minimums

- Review at least 40 rendered replies
- No unacceptable replies
- Minimum per-dimension score: 3
- Minimum per-family average: 3

Hard-safety failures cannot be overridden by human review.

Tooling: `app/evaluation/profile_testbot/qualification/human_review_coworker.py`
